How a lab works

Predict, play, prove.

Every lab follows the same three steps, built around how examiners award marks: intuition first, then the exact wording that scores.

01

Predict

Commit to an answer before anything moves. A wrong guess is where the idea sticks.

e.g. Double the launch speed — does the flight time halve, stay the same, or double?
02

Play

Change variables, pause time, take readings. Graphs and equations update as you go.

Record your own results table and plot it, as in a practical paper.
03

Prove

Build a full-mark answer from mark-scheme keywords, then try past-paper questions.

“Horizontal velocity is constant; gravity acts vertically.”
