§2  How a lab works

Predict, play, prove.

Every lab follows the same three steps, built around how examiners award marks: intuition first, then the exact wording that scores.

  1. i.

    Predict

    Commit to an answer before anything moves. A wrong guess is where the idea sticks.

    Example

    Double the launch speed. Does the flight time halve, stay the same, or double?

    HalvesSameDoubles

    Play

    Change variables, pause time, take readings. Graphs and equations update as you go.
