15.1Drugs

Chemicals that change how the body works

A drug is anything taken into the body that alters a chemical reaction in it. Medicinal drugs treat disease or relieve symptoms — painkillers, antibiotics, vaccines and many more.

1

Understand

Three sections

15.1.1

What a drug is

Definition A drug is any substance taken into the body that modifies or affects chemical reactions in the body.

  • drugs can be medicinal (used to treat illness) or recreational (used for their effect on mood)
  • many affect the nervous system, for example at synapses

15.1.3

Summary and key terms

  • a drug modifies chemical reactions in the body
  • medicinal drugs treat or prevent disease, or relieve symptoms

15.2Drugs

Antibiotics, and bacteria that fight back

Antibiotics kill bacteria by attacking structures our cells do not have. But each time they are used, any resistant bacteria survive and multiply — resistance is evolution happening now.

1

Understand

Four sections

15.2.1

How antibiotics work

  • antibiotics kill bacteria or stop them growing
  • they target structures or processes found in bacteria but not in human cells — for example, the bacterial cell wall — so they harm the bacteria without harming us
15.2.2

Why antibiotics do not affect viruses Supp.

  • viruses are not cells: they have no cell wall and no metabolism of their own
  • they reproduce inside host cells, using the cell’s own machinery
  • so there is nothing for an antibiotic to target
15.2.3

Antibiotic resistance

  • some bacteria are resistant to antibiotics — for example, MRSA is resistant to many
  • Supp. resistance arises by random mutation; when an antibiotic is used, the non-resistant bacteria die, but the resistant ones survive and reproduce, passing on the resistance allele — natural selection
  • to reduce the development of resistance:
    • use antibiotics only when essential — not for viral infections or mild illness
    • finish the course so all the bacteria are killed
    • limit their use in farming
15.2.4

Summary and key terms

  • antibiotics kill bacteria, not viruses
  • resistant bacteria survive and multiply; limit use to slow resistance
Antibiotic
a drug that kills bacteria or stops their growth
Antibiotic resistance
the ability of bacteria to survive an antibiotic that would normally kill them
MRSA
a strain of bacteria resistant to many antibiotics
